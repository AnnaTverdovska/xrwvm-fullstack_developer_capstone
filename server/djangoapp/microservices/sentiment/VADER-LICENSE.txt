vader_lexicon.zip in this folder contains the VADER sentiment lexicon (vader_lexicon.txt) by
C.J. Hutto and Eric Gilbert, in the version distributed with NLTK's data packages. It came with
IBM Skills Network's template unchanged. VADER: https://github.com/cjhutto/vaderSentiment

Hutto, C.J. & Gilbert, E.E. (2014). VADER: A Parsimonious Rule-based Model for Sentiment
Analysis of Social Media Text. Eighth International Conference on Weblogs and Social Media
(ICWSM-14). Ann Arbor, MI, June 2014.
